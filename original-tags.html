Teacher notes and complete dialogue

This lesson uses original fictional characters and situations. It is not taken from films or series. Speech is synthesised, and voice quality depends on the device. It is best for noticing grammatical forms; model rising/falling intonation yourself rather than relying on synthesised speech.

Each scene lasts 30 seconds in timed mode: context 6s, incomplete line 5s, teaching pause 7s, answer and reply 6s, explanation 6s. Extra holds and replays extend the lesson. The timer pauses when the tab is hidden. No exam score is assigned.

After watching: pairs write three new lines, including one negative statement with a positive tag. Each partner gives a natural reply. Challenge: say the same tag once to check information and once to seek agreement.
