Language toolkit

Narrative: was/were + -ing for background; past simple for events; had + past participle for an earlier past event.

Habits: used to + infinitive; would for repeated past actions with a clear past context; be used to + noun/-ing for familiarity.

Polite questions: Could you tell me what time the train leaves? Could you tell me if it’s direct?

Pair challenge: explain one answer without naming the rule. Can your partner work it out?
